Privacy and data

Last updated: 21 August 2026

SpoonBuddy is made by Jaq Kennedy in the United Kingdom. This page sets out what the app stores, where it goes, and what never leaves your device. It is written to be read rather than to be legally impressive, and it says what actually happens.

The short version

Your activity log lives on your own device. If you turn syncing on, a copy also sits in a Google Firebase database so that your phone and your computer agree with each other. There are no adverts, no advertising identifiers, no analytics and no crash reporting. Nothing is sold or handed to anyone for marketing.

What is stored on your device

  • The activities you track: what you did, when you started and stopped, and the spoon cost attached to each one.
  • Your settings: your daily spoon budget, your list of activities and their costs, and how the app is set up.
  • Nothing else. The app does not read your files, contacts, calendar or location.

Signing in

To sync between devices, the app needs a way to tell which data is yours, so you sign in with an email address and a password. Both are handled by Firebase Authentication, which is Google's service. Your password is stored by Firebase in a form nobody can read back, and it never reaches me.

Your email address is stored by Firebase so that signing in on a second device brings you back to the same data. Being the developer, I can see the list of registered addresses in the Firebase console. I do not use it for anything: there is no mailing list, no newsletter, and I will not write to you unless you have written to me first.

What is stored in the cloud, if you turn syncing on

Syncing is off until you switch it on. With it on, the activity log and settings described above are held in Firebase Cloud Firestore, in Google's London region (europe-west2), so the data stays in the UK.

The lawful basis, if you like that sort of thing, is that the data is processed to provide a service you have asked for. Delete your account and it goes.

Sending feedback

The Windows app has a feedback button. Pressing it opens your own email program with a message ready for you to read, edit and send. The app itself transmits nothing, and nothing is sent until you send it.

The message contains what you write, plus the app version, your Windows and PowerShell versions, your settings (theme, whether sync is on, the category you are pacing along with its limits, and your chart span) and two counts: how many entries and how many activities you have. Note that the category name is one you typed yourself, so it will appear as you wrote it.

It does not include your entries, any times, any activity names, your email address or any account identifier.

A copy is saved on your own computer at %LOCALAPPDATA%\SpoonBuddy\spoonbuddy-feedback.txt and placed on your clipboard, so you can still send the report if no email program is set up. Neither copy leaves your computer. Feedback arrives at spoonbuddy+support@ik.me and is kept in that inbox while the issue is open.

The Android app has no feedback button.

What is never collected

  • Adverts and advertising identifiers. None, at all.
  • Analytics of any kind. The app does not report that you opened it, how long you used it, or which buttons you pressed.
  • Crash reporting. If it falls over, I only know because someone tells me.
  • Your name, address, phone number or date of birth.
  • Location.
  • Health records or diagnoses. The app has no idea why your day costs what it costs, and does not ask.

Paying for sync

Any purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Google takes the payment and the app is told only whether it has been made. Card details never reach SpoonBuddy or me.

Deleting your account and your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything held on that device.

To have your account and its synced data deleted, email spoonbuddy+privacy@ik.me from the address you signed up with, or tell me which address to remove. I will delete the account and the data attached to it, and confirm when it is done.

Under UK data protection law you can also ask for a copy of what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or object to it being held at all. Same address, and there is no charge.

Children

SpoonBuddy is not designed for or aimed at children under 13, and is not marketed to them.

Changes to this page

If what the app does changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves. While the app is still in testing, that may happen fairly often.

Contact

For anything privacy related, spoonbuddy+privacy@ik.me. For everything else, spoonbuddy+support@ik.me. One person reads both, so it may not be instant, but it is read.
