    SpoonBuddy

    Know what you've got left, without being told off for it.

    SpoonBuddy is an energy pacing app built on spoon theory. You tell it what you're doing. It works out what that costs you, and keeps a running total of what's left for the day.

    The balance is allowed to go below zero. When it does, the app calls it borrowing from tomorrow, because that is what it is, and because plenty of days give you no other option. A bad night's sleep starts you lower the next morning for the same reason.

    There's no streak to keep and no score to beat. Nothing in it will congratulate you for pushing through. If you'd like the longer explanation, Squish does a better job of it than I do.

    Get it

    Android

    SpoonBuddy is on Google Play, in closed testing. That means it is not public yet and you cannot find it by searching; you join the testers and install it from there.

    Google asks for twelve testers, signed up for fourteen days in a row, before an app like this can be released properly. So joining is a real favour and not a formality.

    It costs testers nothing. The app is paid on the store, but testers go through a test purchase that takes no money.

    Ask to join the testers

    Ask to join the group, and the opt-in link comes back to you. You'll need an Android phone, and the Google account your Play Store is signed in with. The test is open in the UK, US and Canada at the moment; if you're somewhere else, say so and it can be opened up.

    Windows

    The desktop version is free, and always will be. It's early and rough in places, so please don't rely on it for anything that matters yet.

    Windows (.zip)

    It downloads straight away and installs by hand; instructions come with it. Your antivirus may warn you about it, because the certificate belongs to a developer it has never heard of rather than because anything is wrong. Everything is also on the releases page if you'd rather see the list. If you get stuck partway through, tell me which step you reached and I'll pick it up from there.

    What it costs

    The Windows app is free. The Android app is paid, once, with no subscription and nothing held back behind a further payment. While it's in closed testing, testers pay nothing.

    If money is tight, write to spoonbuddy+support@ik.me and ask for a free copy. There's no form and nothing to prove. That offer is not a gesture; it's the reason the app is priced the way it is rather than carved into a free half and a paid half.

    Get in touch

    Anything confusing, ugly, broken, or that made you feel judged. That last one above all else. Write to spoonbuddy+support@ik.me, or use the report button inside the app if that's easier.
