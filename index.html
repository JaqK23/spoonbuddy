    SpoonBuddy

    Know what you've got left, without being told off for it.

    SpoonBuddy is an energy pacing app built on spoon theory. You tell it what you're doing. It works out what that costs you, and keeps a running total of what's left for the day.

    The balance is allowed to go below zero. When it does, the app calls it borrowing from tomorrow, because that is what it is, and because plenty of days give you no other option. A bad night's sleep starts you lower the next morning for the same reason.

    There's no streak to keep and no score to beat. Nothing in it will congratulate you for pushing through. If you'd like the longer explanation, Squish does a better job of it than I do.

    Get it

    SpoonBuddy is at the alpha stage, which means early, rough and changing often. Things will look unfinished and some of them won't work. Please don't rely on it for anything that matters yet.

    Windows (.zip) Android (.apk)

    Both files download straight away. Neither is in an app store yet, so both need installing by hand. Instructions come with the download. If you get stuck partway through, tell me which step you reached and I'll pick it up from there. Everything is also on the releases page if you would rather see the list.

    What it costs

    Nothing at the moment. While it's in testing, both versions are free.

    When the Android app reaches the Play Store properly there will be a charge on it. I'll say what that is well before it happens, and testers won't be caught out by it.

    Get in touch

    Anything confusing, ugly, broken, or that made you feel judged. That last one above all else. Write to spoonbuddy+support@ik.me, or use the report button inside the app if that's easier.

    Spoon theory was coined by Christine Miserandino. SpoonBuddy has no connection to her or to anyone else. It's one person's app.
